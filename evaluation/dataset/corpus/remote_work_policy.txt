Acme Robotics — Remote Work Policy (Internal)

This document describes Acme Robotics' policy on remote and hybrid work
arrangements, effective for all full-time employees.

Eligibility: Employees who have completed at least 90 days of continuous
employment are eligible to request a remote or hybrid work arrangement.
New hires during their first 90 days are expected to work on-site to
support onboarding.

Work Location: Employees on a hybrid arrangement are expected to be present
in the office a minimum of two days per week. Fully remote arrangements
require approval from both the employee's manager and the department head.

Equipment: Acme Robotics provides a laptop and a one-time home office stipend
of 500 dollars for employees approved for remote work. This stipend can be
used for items such as a desk, chair, or monitor.

Working Hours: Employees are expected to be available during core hours of
10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in their local time zone, regardless of work location.
Outside of core hours, flexible scheduling is permitted as long as weekly
deliverables are met.

Paid Time Off: Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per year,
in addition to 10 company holidays. PTO requests should be submitted through
the HR portal at least two weeks in advance where possible.

Expense Reimbursement: Reasonable expenses related to remote work, such as
internet costs up to 50 dollars per month, may be submitted for reimbursement
through the finance team with appropriate receipts.

Review: This policy is reviewed annually by the People Operations team and
is subject to change with 30 days' notice to employees.
